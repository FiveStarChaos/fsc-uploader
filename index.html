Five Star Chaos Uploader

Five Star Chaos Uploader is a private tool used by the owner of the Five Star Chaos channel to publish short gameplay clips to their own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

What it does

  • Uploads short videos that the channel owner has reviewed and approved to the owner's own YouTube channel (using the YouTube Data API) and Instagram account (using the Instagram Graph API).
  • Sets each video's title, description and hashtags.
  • Runs only on the owner's computer. It has one user, the channel owner, and is not offered to the public.

Content and credit

Clips are made from streamers' gameplay with their permission, and every clip credits the original creator.
