Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Five Star Chaos Uploader ("the app") is a private tool that the owner of the Five Star Chaos channel uses to upload their own videos to their own YouTube channel and Instagram account. The channel owner is the app's only user.

Use of YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos (the youtube.upload scope). The app only uploads videos the owner has approved, to the owner's own channel. It does not read, change or delete other videos, comments, subscribers or account details.
  • Instagram: permission to publish Reels to the owner's own Instagram professional account through the Instagram Graph API.

What the app stores

The sign-in tokens Google and Meta issue are stored only on the owner's own computer, so the app can upload without signing in each time. Video files and their titles and descriptions are also kept on that computer. The app has no server and no database, and it collects no data about viewers or any other person.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share any data. Videos and their details are sent only to YouTube and Instagram, when the owner publishes them.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access to a Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and to an Instagram or Facebook account in its Apps and websites settings. Deleting the token files on the owner's computer also removes the app's stored access.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
